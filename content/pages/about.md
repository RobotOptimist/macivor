## What does MacIvor Technical LLC do?

Established in 2018, MacIvor Technical LLC started as a technical consulting business.
It was created to provide application development services to a startup called SafeRx Solutions LLC.

MacIvor Technical LLC built software to help patients struggling with prescribed narcotics. The software did this by helping clinicians at SafeRx Solutions monitor and document patient compliance with prescriptions.

Since then, the company went on hiatus from 2021 to 2025.

In mid-2026, the company came back with a new focus. The company creates applications on various platforms, such as Discord, Reddit, iOS, and Android. The company would focus on researching and building apps that brought satisfaction and joy to users.

This starts with the new flagship application for Discord: [Stock Market Replay](https://stock-market-replay.com).

Additionally, MacIvor Technical LLC provides expertise and experience to potential customers who want to create software, but need help getting started or just want something built for them.
